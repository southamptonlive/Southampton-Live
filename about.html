About Soton Live

Soton Live is Southampton's gig guide: every gig, club night, comedy show and theatre date across Greater Southampton, refreshed automatically, credited to its source, with no fake anything. Built in Southampton, for the scene.

Our data promise

"No fake data" cuts both ways: we don't put made-up numbers in front of you, and we don't take real ones from you without saying exactly why.

What's public: your display name, and anything you choose to post on the board. That's it.

What's private: your email, the first part of your postcode if you gave it, and what you save and follow. Never shown to anyone, never sold, never shared as yours.

What we publish: combined, anonymised statistics — always groups of 10 or more, never more detailed than the first part of a postcode. Things like "a third of Southampton's gig-goers travel in from outside the city centre." We publish them free, for the scene: venues use them to win funding, and we'll share them with the council, researchers and organisations fighting for grassroots music, like the Music Venue Trust.

What we'll never do: sell your data, share your individual details, or run tracking scripts and cookies. The site has none.

Your controls: skip the postcode entirely; remove it later from your profile; delete your account and everything goes with it. Ask us for a copy of your data any time at hello@southampton.live.

Why bother? Because you can't save what you can't count. Venues are closing across the country partly because their audiences are invisible on paper. Ten seconds to type the first part of your postcode helps make Southampton's audience impossible to ignore.
