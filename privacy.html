Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Soton Live is Southampton's gig guide at southampton.live, operated by [YOUR LEGAL / TRADING NAME] ("we"), the data controller, registered with the ICO (registration number: [ICO NUMBER — arriving]). This page explains what personal data we hold and why, in plain English. We collect as little as possible.

Browsing without an account

You can use the whole guide without an account. We set no tracking cookies and run no advertising trackers. Your saves, follows and reminders are stored on your own device only.

If you create an account

We store: your email address (to sign you in — we use magic links — email sign-in links — so we never store passwords), your display name (public, shown on your posts and gig submissions), and your city/town and postcode (private — never shown to anyone; used to understand where our audience is). If you follow venues or acts, save events, post on the scene board, submit gigs or claim a venue/artist profile, that content is stored with your account.

Cookies

We don't set cookies. No tracking cookies, no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies — which is why you won't see a cookie banner here. The site uses your device's own storage (localStorage) for things you've asked it to remember: your saves, follows, reminders and, if you have an account, your signed-in session. That storage never leaves your device except to sync your own account data, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Where it lives and who processes it

Account data is stored with Supabase in their London (UK) region. Sign-in emails are delivered by Resend, a US company; that transfer is covered by Resend's data-processing agreement incorporating the UK's approved safeguards. Our hosting (SiteGround) and Supabase keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security, retained briefly. If you claim a venue or artist profile, we also store the evidence link and message you provide — which may include a work email address — until the claim is resolved. We process account data on the lawful basis of performing our contract with you; any future mailer will be strictly separate opt-in consent. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from your Profile — this permanently removes your account, posts, submissions, follows and saves. You can export your data from Profile too. For anything else (access, correction, complaints) email hello@southampton.live. You also have the right to complain to the UK regulator, the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Retention & age

We keep account data while your account exists and delete it when you do. Soton Live is for ages 13 and over.
