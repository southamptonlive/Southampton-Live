Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Southampton.Live is Southampton's gig guide at southampton.live, operated by Injekt Media ("we"), the data controller, registered with the ICO (registration number: 00015385667). This page explains what personal data we hold and why, in plain English. We collect as little as possible.

Browsing without an account

You can use the whole guide without an account. We set no advertising cookies and run no ad networks or trackers. Your saves, follows and reminders are stored on your own device only.

If you create an account

We store: your email address (to sign you in — we use magic links — email sign-in links — so we never store passwords), your display name (public, shown on your posts and gig submissions), and — only if you choose to give it — the first part of your postcode (the "postcode district", e.g. SO15; private, used only as described under Anonymised statistics below). We also record how you use the site — events saved, followed, clicked through and submitted — to make the product work and for the same anonymised statistics. If you follow venues or acts, save events, post on the scene board, submit gigs or claim a venue/artist profile, that content is stored with your account.

Anonymised statistics

If you choose to give it, we use the first part of your postcode (the "postcode district", e.g. SO15) together with how you use the site (events saved, followed, clicked through and submitted) to produce combined, anonymised statistics about Southampton's live events audience — for example, how far people travel to gigs, or which parts of the city the scene reaches. These statistics are always built from groups of at least 10 people and are designed so that no one can be identified. We may publish them, or share them with venues, funders, researchers, public bodies and partners working to support live events. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: supporting Southampton's grassroots scene and planning our service.

Giving a postcode is optional. We store only the first part (e.g. SO15), never your full postcode, and you can remove it from your account at any time. Because our published statistics are anonymous, they are not personal data and are unaffected if you delete your account; everything that identifies you is deleted with your account.

Cookies & analytics

Essential storage only, by default. The site uses your device's own storage (localStorage) for things you've asked it to remember: your saves, follows, reminders, your filter and cookie choices and, if you have an account, your signed-in session. That storage never leaves your device except to sync your own account data, and clearing your browser data removes it. We set no advertising cookies and run no ad networks or trackers.

Analytics — only if you say yes. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visitors and see which parts of the guide get used, so we can state an honest audience figure and improve the site. It runs only after you choose "Accept analytics" on the one-time banner; until then no analytics script loads and no analytics cookie is set. If you choose "No thanks", nothing is set and the site works identically. Your lawful basis for this is consent, which you can withdraw at any time via the "Cookies" link in the footer.

What analytics collects when accepted: pages and events viewed, filters used, outbound ticket clicks (which outlet, which listing), device and browser type, approximate location derived from a truncated IP address, and a random identifier stored in a first-party cookie (_ga, up to 2 years) so repeat visits can be counted. Google's advertising features, signals and ad personalisation are switched off. We never send your email, display name, postcode or anything else that identifies you. Google processes this data as our processor under its Ads Data Processing Terms, with transfers to the US covered by the UK's approved safeguards.

Where it lives and who processes it

Account data is stored with Supabase in their London (UK) region. Sign-in emails are delivered by Resend, a US company; that transfer is covered by Resend's data-processing agreement incorporating the UK's approved safeguards. Our hosting (SiteGround) and Supabase keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security, retained briefly. If you claim a venue or artist profile, we also store the evidence link and message you provide — which may include a work email address — until the claim is resolved. We process account data on the lawful basis of performing our contract with you; any future mailer will be strictly separate opt-in consent. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from your Profile — this permanently removes your account, posts, submissions, follows and saves. You can export your data from Profile too. For anything else (access, correction, complaints) email hello@southampton.live. You also have the right to complain to the UK regulator, the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Retention & age

We keep account data while your account exists and delete it when you do. Accounts, posting and claims are for adults (18 or over); anyone can read the guide without an account. Users can object to inclusion in anonymised statistics at any time via their profile settings or hello@southampton.live.
