Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Soton Live is Southampton's gig guide at southampton.live. This page explains what personal data we hold and why, in plain English. We collect as little as possible.

Browsing without an account

You can use the whole guide without an account. We set no tracking cookies and run no advertising trackers. Your saves, follows and reminders are stored on your own device only.

If you create an account

We store: your email address (to sign you in — we use one-tap sign-in links, so we never store passwords), your display name (public, shown on your posts and gig submissions), and your city/town and postcode (private — never shown to anyone; used to understand where our audience is). If you follow venues or acts, save events, post on the scene board, submit gigs or claim a venue/artist profile, that content is stored with your account.

Where it lives and who processes it

Account data is stored with Supabase in their London (UK/EU) region. Sign-in emails are delivered by Resend. Both act as data processors under their standard data-processing agreements. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from your Profile — this permanently removes your account, posts, submissions, follows and saves. You can export your data from Profile too. For anything else (access, correction, complaints) email hello@southampton.live. You also have the right to complain to the UK regulator, the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Retention & age

We keep account data while your account exists and delete it when you do. Soton Live is for ages 13 and over.
