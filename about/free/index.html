How this stays free

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Southampton.Live is free to use and free to list on. Nobody pays to see what's on, and no venue, promoter or artist will ever pay to be included. Here's how that works, in plain English.

The rule everything else follows

No fake data, ever. Nothing on this site is invented — no made-up events, no guessed prices, no "doors probably 7pm," no manufactured hype. If we don't know something, we show nothing rather than a guess. Every listing says where it came from and links there. This page follows the same rule: it describes how we actually run, and if that changes, this page changes first.

Free listings, for good

Listing on Southampton.Live is free for every venue, promoter, artist and collective — permanently. We've made that commitment publicly and we'll keep it. Claiming your venue page is free too, and always will be. If we ever build extra paid tools for venues, the free listing and the free page stay exactly as they are.

Your data

Your display name is public. Everything else is private: your email, what you save, who you follow, and the first part of your postcode if you chose to give it. We never sell or share anyone's individual data. The only statistics we publish are combined and anonymised — groups of ten or more, never finer than the first part of a postcode — built to help Southampton's venues prove their audiences exist.

Analytics only happens if you say yes. You'll see one plain question — accept or no thanks, two equal buttons. Decline and no analytics cookie is set, and the site works exactly the same. You can change your mind any time from the footer.

How we earn

Running the guide costs real money and real hours, so we earn it in ways that don't cost you anything and don't bend the listings:

  • One ad slot. A single, clearly labelled, static ad — a local business relevant to a night out, never an ad network, never anything that tracks you. We turn down what doesn't fit.
  • Ticket links. Tickets are always bought from the official seller. Some of those links pay us a small commission — the price you pay is identical either way, and a commission never changes what we list or the order you see it in.
  • Sponsorship. Where a sponsor supports the site or a section, it says so, visibly. Sponsors get named; they don't get a say in listings.
  • Venue tools, later. We may offer optional paid extras for claimed venues. Listings and claims stay free regardless.

What we never do

  • Sell tickets or hold your money — every sale happens with the seller, where your consumer rights live.
  • Invent a listing, a price, a time or a review.
  • Sell the order of the page. Ads are labelled; everything else is chronology and your filters.
  • Scrape around a block. When a source says no, we ask instead.
  • Sell your data, or run ad networks and trackers.

Hold us to it

This page is the deal. If anything here stops being true, tell us — hello@southampton.live — and we'll fix the site or fix this page, whichever is wrong.
